BLACKPINK Announces Global World Tour Extensions for Late 2026

Following the massive success of their recent stadium shows, the group has officially confirmed 15 additional dates across North America and Europe.

ED

By BLINKCO Editorial

3 Min Read

In an unexpected but highly anticipated announcement, BLACKPINK's management has confirmed that the group will be extending their record-breaking world tour through the end of 2026. The new leg of the tour promises to bring an upgraded setlist and stage design that fans have never seen before.

A Monumental Year for the Group

The past year has been nothing short of phenomenal for the quartet. With the release of their critically acclaimed album and their subsequent stadium tours selling out in minutes, the demand from international fans has skyrocketed. Addressing this, the group decided to add 15 more cities to their schedule.

"We wanted to make sure we reached as many BLINKs as possible. Your energy during the last leg was incredible, and we can't wait to share these new stages with you."

The new dates include stops in cities they haven't visited in over four years, alongside encore performances in major metropolitan hubs like New York, London, and Paris. According to insiders, the production team has been working tirelessly to revamp the concert experience, incorporating state-of-the-art visual mapping and interactive fan segments.

Ticketing Details

Official BLINK fanclub members will have priority access during the early pre-sale period starting next Monday. General public sales will open later in the week. Given the unprecedented demand, fans are advised to ensure their memberships are up-to-date and to register on the official ticketing platform beforehand.

Quick Tip

Ensure you are logged into your BLINKCO account and have linked your membership ID before the pre-sale begins to avoid any delays at checkout.

Stay tuned to the BLINKCO news portal for the official list of cities and exact ticketing schedules. Let's make this extended tour the most unforgettable experience yet!
